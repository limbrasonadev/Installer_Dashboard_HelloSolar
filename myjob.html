Installer Field Guidelines & Safety SOP (Click to expand)

Adherence to DOLE-OSH and PCAB rooftop solar safety standards is mandatory across all Hello Solar job sites.

1. Pre-Arrival & Fall Protection
Inspect full-body harnesses, anchor points, and lifeline ropes before rooftop ascent. Never work during active rain or wind gusts exceeding 30 km/h.
2. Structural Mounting & Torque
Align mounting rails to roof structural purlins. Apply UV-rated weatherproofing to all penetrations. Use calibrated torque wrenches (14 Nm clamp torque).
3. DC Stringing & Polarity
Confirm string Voc and open-circuit polarity with calibrated multimeter before connecting DC isolator. Keep cables in rigid conduits away from roof moisture.
4. Handover & Client Briefing
Affix emergency disconnect placards. Connect monitoring app to client Wi-Fi. Provide 5-minute rapid shutdown orientation and secure digital sign-off.
