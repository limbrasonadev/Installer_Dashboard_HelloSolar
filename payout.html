1. BIR Revenue Regulation RR 2-98
Payments to PCAB registered contractors and solar technical teams are subject to a mandatory 2% expanded creditable withholding tax deducted at source.
2. Quarterly BIR Form 2307
Hello Solar issues your signed BIR Form 2307 Certificates every calendar quarter. You can use these certificates directly as tax credits against your BIR quarterly filings.
3. Regular Disbursement Cycle
Approved milestone payouts are processed twice weekly. Funds reflect directly in your registered bank account via direct electronic bank clearing.
